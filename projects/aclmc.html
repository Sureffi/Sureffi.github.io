Overview_

ACLMC is a research project exploring deep learning approaches for non-stationary financial time-series prediction. The goal was to build and evaluate models that can handle the fundamental challenges of market data — regime shifts, non-stationarity, and the gap between offline metrics and real-world performance.

Built complete pipelines for financial data: labeling strategies, feature extraction, and dataset generation from raw market data into model-ready formats.

Developed and compared multiple architectures — LSTM for sequential dependencies, ConvLSTM for spatiotemporal patterns, and attention-based models for learning which time steps and features matter most for prediction.

A significant focus was analyzing failure modes and regime shifts — understanding why models that perform well on historical data break down in production, and what structural properties of market data make this an inherently difficult problem.

Features_

Data Pipelines

End-to-end pipelines for financial time-series: labeling, feature extraction, normalization, and dataset generation from raw OHLCV and indicator data.

LSTM Models

Recurrent architectures for capturing sequential dependencies in price action. Multiple configurations tested for look-back windows and hidden dimensions.

ConvLSTM

Hybrid convolutional-recurrent models for capturing spatiotemporal patterns across multiple features and time steps simultaneously.

Attention Mechanisms

Attention-based architectures that learn which time steps and features matter most for prediction, improving interpretability and performance.

Failure Mode Analysis

Systematic analysis of regime shifts and non-stationarity. Understanding why offline accuracy fails to transfer to live markets.

Robustness Testing

Evaluating model performance beyond standard metrics — testing against unseen market regimes and analyzing degradation patterns.
